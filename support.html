Help with True

Need to talk to someone right now?

  • Childline (up to 19): call 0800 1111
  • Shout: text SHOUT to 85258
  • Samaritans: call 116 123
  • Papyrus HOPELINE247 (under 35): call 0800 068 4141
  • In danger right now: call 999

You can reach these from inside True too, even from the lock screen.

I forgot my code

Tap "Forgot your code?" on the lock screen. If you turned on backup, type the recovery code you saved and choose a new code. Everything comes back.

If you never turned on backup, nobody can unlock True for you, including us. That's what keeps your words private if someone else picks up your phone. You can start fresh, but that deletes everything in True on that phone.

Someone saw my recovery code

Anyone with your recovery code can open your story on their own phone, so change it. In About Me, under Backup, tap "Get a new code". Your old code stops working straight away. Save the new one somewhere only you can get to.

I've got a new phone

Install True and tap "I had True before" on the first screen. Type your recovery code and your story comes back. This only works if you turned on backup on your old phone.

Can anyone else read what I write?

No. Your words stay on your phone, locked with your code. Carers, social workers, schools and the people who make True can't see them. There's no adult login. Read our privacy page for the details.

Unlock with Face ID or fingerprint

Turn it on in About Me (tap your initial, top right), under "On this phone". If someone adds a new face or fingerprint to the phone, True turns this off by itself and asks for your code. That stops anyone adding theirs to get in.

Taking my story with me

"Download my everything" in About Me saves all your check-ins, writing, milestones and letters in one file on your phone. It's yours to keep.

Deleting everything

Turn off backup in About Me to delete the online copy. Then "Forgot your code?" → "I don't have a recovery code" → "Start fresh" wipes True from your phone. Deleting the app does that too.

Something else

Email hello@trueapp.co.uk. Please don't send anything private you've written in True. We don't need it to help you.
