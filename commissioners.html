For commissioners

True is for the young person. That is the point.

True is a private self-awareness app for looked-after young people aged 10–21. They check in, write, keep milestones, and take their own story with them when they leave care.

What you buy is not access. There is no carer login, no social-worker dashboard, and no feed of journals. If an adult could read the words, they would not be worth writing.

What the young person gets

  • A PIN-locked space that lives on their phone
  • Mood check-ins, a journal, letters to future self, milestones with photos or voice notes
  • A leaving-care pack they generate themselves — not a redacted case file
  • Optional encrypted backup so a lost phone is not a lost story
  • Signposting to Childline, Shout, Samaritans and Papyrus, including from the lock screen

What you will never get

AskAnswer
Can staff read entries?No. Not built. Not available later as an add-on.
Can we pull identifiable data?No names, no journals, no photos on our side unless they turn backup on — and that copy is unreadable ciphertext.
Can we see who is “using it”?Not today. Any future aggregate would be opt-in counts only (for example “checked in 3 days this week”), never text.
Is there AI reading them?No. Tried on-device, cut, will not return without evidence it can read understatement.

Why a council or agency would still fund it

  • Emotional literacy and a continuous record owned by the child — the file they usually receive at 18 is someone else’s notes.
  • Placement moves smash phones and accounts. Backup and export are built for that, not for monitoring.
  • Aligns with Working Together expectations to hear the child’s voice without turning that voice into compliance.
  • Pupil Premium Plus and Virtual School work already pay for tools that improve self-advocacy. This is one the young person will actually open.
